Katathani AR Collection System — Privacy

This workspace is for approved Katathani accounts-receivable staff. Signing in with Google provides your Google account identity and email so the system can verify the administrator’s access list and permitted hotel regions.

The administrator records your staff name. The workspace records who performs business actions, including billing and invoice tracking changes, so colleagues can review activity. Access to financial records is limited by the permissions assigned to each approved account.

Google sign-in does not grant access to your personal Gmail messages or Google Drive files. The workspace’s business Gmail and Drive connections are separately authorized. Email is prepared and sent only through staff actions.

Login information is stored for the current browser tab. Business records and action history are retained separately from login sessions, including when access is removed. Contact the administrator for questions about access, stored personal information, or removal requests.
