## Precise unfinished data requirements

1. **Complete daily invoice entries, including invoices created and cleared between refreshes.** `readVerifiedAccount()` reads open history first. Even when it fetches all zero-inclusive history, it adds only current/printed open rows and zero rows for previously saved missing identities. Unseen historical zero rows are not imported as a daily-entry dataset. There is no date-bounded history method in `OperaReader`, no historical-entry coverage watermark, and no daily-entry report mode. The existing `balance=All` discovery is useful infrastructure but does not fill this gap. [Reader lines 52–102](../worker/refresh/read-snapshot.ts), [client](../worker/opera/client.ts).

2. **OPERA receipt/payment transaction ledger and reporting.** History payment rows participate in pagination/identity audits and are then discarded. `AccountSnapshot` contains an account and invoices, not a persisted payment collection. The normalizer's `applied_amount` comes from `Invoice.payments`, a cumulative invoice snapshot. It is neither a receipt event nor a daily applied-payment event. [Snapshot type and normalization](../worker/opera/normalize.ts), [refresh publisher](../supabase/migrations/20260908170335_ar_compression_context.sql).

3. **Verified Invoice ↔ Payment links, with honest date semantics.** The application does not call the applied-payments GET below. No payment-link dataset, application-event date, unapplication history, reversal pairing, or transfer exclusion is persisted. A current mapping snapshot can support “currently applied”; it cannot by itself prove “applied today.” The owner has resolved the business choice to show receipts and applied amounts separately; the remaining blocker is source evidence, not another preference question.

4. **Cleared/reopened observation history and Needs Review.** Current source state can become cleared/verified again, but publication overwrites the latest row and does not preserve a dedicated verified nonzero → zero → nonzero transition history. `synced_at` is a last observation timestamp, not an exact clearance/payment date. Add observation intervals and the owner-confirmed reopened review flag while keeping the original workflow history. No amount drop should generate a receipt.

5. **Reports integration and truthful coverage labels.** Add distinct daily-entry, OPERA receipt, invoice-application, unallocated snapshot, reversal, and cleared-observation views once their sources pass validation. Existing Hotel/Account Type/Account filters and pagination are reusable. The current Reports “Not yet available” copy still lists Remittance despite its completed separate workspace; update that stale copy/link without waiting for financial ingestion. The original-versus-current invoice-entry amount label is still an open historical definition: preserve both source fields now and show explicit labels rather than silently choosing Open as invoice value. [Product specification §§8/11/13](PRODUCT_SPEC.md).

### Field meanings and critical limits

From `aRPaymentType`/`accountPaymentInfoType`, retain only the needed business fields: exact scoped `transactionNo`, `hotelId`, `transactionCode`, `amount`, `amountUsed`, `balance`, `transactionDate`, `postingDate`, `revenueDate`, `transferDate`, `transferredIn`, and `transferredOut`. Monetary objects carry `amount` and `currencyCode`; enforce THB and preserve exact signed decimals. Do not ingest `paymentCard` or cashier personal details for statistics.

- `amount` is the source payment amount; `amountUsed` is the amount already applied; `balance` is the remaining unallocated amount. The latter two are current state unless the environment proves an event representation.
- `transactionDate` on a payment is documented as the payment's date. Some payment schema descriptions for posting/revenue/transfer dates still refer to an invoice, so the labels alone do not establish a reliable application or receipt date. Verify them against the OPERA UI/source report around business-date rollover.
- `aRAppliedPaymentType.transactionDate`, `postingDate`, and `closeDate` are inherited invoice fields. Its `appliedAmount` and `paymentTrxNo` establish candidate linkage; there is **no explicit `applicationDate`, application event ID, or reversal-of ID** in that definition. Never use its invoice date as the payment-application date.
- `aRInvoiceType` includes `originalAmount`, `amount`, `payments`, `balance`, `transactionDate`, `postingDate`, `closeDate`, `invoiceType`, and compression/transfer context. `invoiceType` distinguishes `Normal`, `Credit`, `OldBalance`, and `PasserBy`. Preserve this distinction for initial import and daily-entry counting; do not turn an opening-balance representation or compressed aggregate into an extra newly issued invoice.

Oracle's [Managing AR Invoice Payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/hospitality/opera-cloud/26.2/ocsuh/t_accounts_receivable_managing_ar_invoice_payments.htm) confirms that unallocated payments can exist without an invoice, unapplied payments return to unallocated status, and a reversal creates a debit payment. It also warns that zero-balance payments require disabling the open-only filter, and that compressed-invoice payments target the parent. Transfers can move part or all of an unallocated payment between accounts. Therefore a payment row, signed amount, or balance reduction alone is insufficient to classify fresh receipts. Editable reference/remark text must not be the only reversal/transfer evidence.

Display the verified metric as **OPERA posted receipts**, not bank-cleared funds. Keep receipt flows, invoice application amounts, current unallocated balances, reversals, and transfers distinct. An unallocated balance “as of refresh” must not be labeled as an unallocated cash flow for a date range. If only a current application mapping can be verified, expose it as current mapping or a clearly labeled receipt-date cohort; leave application-date activity unavailable.

## Bounded validation steps that can proceed without another owner decision

These are proposed read-only steps, not checks executed in this audit. The parent task can perform them through the existing authorized Worker/secret-storage path. No new service, paid subscription, or financial test transaction is required.

1. **Add and test isolated readers first.** Add date-range history and the two detail/mapping GET methods to `OperaReader` with fixed paths, existing credential handling, redirect refusal and bounded responses. Use synthetic fixtures for duplicate IDs, currency, missing dates, negative/reversal signs, compressed rows, backdated records and pagination failure. Do not alter the current reconciliation path while exploring payment semantics.
2. **Check one Account and a short explicit range per hotel.** Start with a 1–2 day window and read every page at the environment-proven size 20. Compare combined Invoices/Payments with each kind separately, and repeat at 10 for stable membership. Compare the first/last day boundaries with nearby unfiltered or narrowly filtered evidence. Record only categorical results, counts, date-field presence and nonreversible digests outside private storage; no customer payloads in Git/logs.
3. **Verify representative existing cases.** Use authorized naturally occurring examples: partial payment, receipt applied across multiple invoices, unallocated receipt, reversal, unapplication, transferred payment, compressed parent, net-zero Account and created-and-cleared invoice. Read exact IDs with `invoicePaymentDetails` and `getAppliedInvoicePayments`. Do not create or reverse real payments to manufacture examples. When a case is absent, retain synthetic tests and mark that live edge unverified rather than claiming coverage.
4. **Prove date and linkage semantics independently.** Match payment `transactionNo` to mapping `paymentTrxNo`; match invoice `transactionNo` within its Hotel/Account. Confirm signed receipt/reversal conventions and transfer handling. Compare `transactionDate`/`postingDate` with the hotel Business Date and source UI. If no authoritative application event date is exposed, do not enable daily applied-activity totals; a dated mapping observation is not a dated financial event.
5. **Prove daily-entry coverage.** Use `balance=All` Account discovery plus zero-inclusive history for the explicit source-date interval, including rows never present in Current. Reconcile exact invoice membership, not just totals. Exclude or separately explain opening balances, credit rows and compressed aggregates. Preserve invoice dates on late discovery; do not count initial import as today's entries.
6. **Publish only completed coverage.** Store a per-hotel/account/kind/range result and advance its verified watermark only after all pages and reconciliation pass. Leave the prior successful dataset intact on errors. A proposed rolling overlap (for example seven days) improves late-data recovery, but is not proof against arbitrary backdating: support reviewed date-range backfills and periodic reconciliation. Show verified coverage dates explicitly.
7. **Add private data and report projections after the proofs.** Use separate receipt records, application links/observations, historical invoice entries and source-balance transitions. Preserve source identity and source dates alongside technical observation time; use exact decimal/satang arithmetic and explicit unknowns. Keep these additive to current snapshot tables. Append source changes rather than silently rewriting historical cash totals.
8. **Complete reopened review handling now.** In atomic publication, detect a previously verified zero returning to verified nonzero, retain original billing/reminder dates/stages, and record a Needs Review transition. This business decision is already confirmed. Add synthetic close/reopen/missing/error cases and verify no duplicate daily invoice entry or automatic send/reset occurs.
