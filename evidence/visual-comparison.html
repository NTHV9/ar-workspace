  • Total Open descending is the default. The reference's older Over 90 sort caption is superseded.
  • Latest sent stages use the new business vocabulary; Final remains urgent while open.
  • Due data unavailable in live mode is not called “Not billed.” OPERA aging is unavailable until source buckets are verified.
  • Review documents, History, Collections, Gmail and Reports are outside this first increment; no dead action buttons pretend they work.
  • Different fictional account counts and amounts; scrolling exposes all accounts without top-N truncation.
